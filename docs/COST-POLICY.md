# Sideline Stats Cost Policy

## Phase 1 hard constraint
Incremental AI/development spend target: **$0**.

Use only capabilities already included in existing subscriptions and existing free allowances.

Do not purchase API credits, enable automatic credit reloads, add a paid service, upgrade infrastructure, or create another recurring development expense without explicit owner approval.

If included AI usage is exhausted, development pauses rather than purchasing additional capacity.

## Permanent rule
Prefer the simplest architecture capable of meeting the approved product requirement. No paid dependency, paid API, infrastructure upgrade, recurring service, or additional AI expenditure may be introduced without explicit owner approval.

## Stop rule
If implementation becomes disproportionately complex relative to the value of the feature, stop and raise the issue for owner review rather than continuing to consume resources.
